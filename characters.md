# Characters

*The art, in three pages:* **Characters** · [Illustrations](illustrations.md) · [Scenes](scenes.md)

Everyone drawn in the site's scenery, by world, with where they turn up and what they say. Each picture is a 1024 px PNG; click it for the vector. The drawings are the same parts listed in [illustrations.md](illustrations.md), so this page cannot drift from the code: all 48 pieces are regenerated by `bash tools/build.sh`.

Nature pieces are drawn in the day palette and Space pieces in the night palette, so the Space characters look best on a dark background. Lines quoted are the ones the scenes actually speak.
