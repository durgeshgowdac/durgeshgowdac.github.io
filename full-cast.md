# Full cast

Every drawing in the site's scenery, in one place: the franchise characters, the site's own creatures and the element marks. Each picture is a 1024 px PNG from `cast/png/`; click it for the vector version in `cast/svg/`. For the same cast grouped on contact sheets, see [cast.md](cast.md).

All 48 pieces are regenerated from the SVG components by `bash tools/cast/build.sh` (see the README). Nature pieces are drawn in the day palette and Space pieces in the night palette, so the Space characters look best on a dark background.
